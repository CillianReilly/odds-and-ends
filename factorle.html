Factorle

How to play

A secret number is hiding. Guess any whole number of 2 or more and Factorle breaks your guess into its prime factors, smallest first, then lines them up against the secret number's factors position by position. Your guess can't have more prime factors (counting repeats) than the secret does — so once you know the secret has, say, four, a guess with five or more won't be accepted. Type a number or build one by tapping primes below, then press Enter to submit it. Any prime that's turned green or yellow must appear in every guess from then on.

3 This prime sits in exactly that position in the secret factorisation.
5 This prime is in the secret factorisation, but somewhere else.
7 This prime doesn't divide the secret number at all.

Every prime under 100 is listed above the guess box. As you guess, each one turns green (confirmed, right position in some guess), yellow (confirmed in the secret, wrong position so far), or dark (confirmed not a factor) — primes you haven't narrowed down yet stay neutral.

The secret number's primes are all under 100, and it can repeat one just like your guess can — if you guess 2 twice but the secret only has one 2, only one of those tiles can earn colour. The arrow at the right of each row tells you whether the secret number has more or fewer prime factors than your guess. Six guesses, then the answer.
